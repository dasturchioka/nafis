## Who, and which goal

### Perpetual intermediates · ui
**Ask:** Is the main path tuned for the person on their twentieth use, rather than for the first-run demo or the rare expert?
**Smell:** Tutorials, welcome steps or explanatory bot messages that repeat on every visit. Screens judged only by the first-time walkthrough. Or a dense expert layout that slows down the everyday user.
**Move:** Optimise the frequent path for the competent regular user, because most people learn just enough and stay at that level. Give beginners a short ramp they soon leave behind, and give experts shortcuts that stay off the main path.
**From:** About Face; Inmates (quoted in "Defending Perpetual Intermediacy")

## Conversations

### Conversation fit · ui
**Ask:** For this person and this goal, is a conversation faster than the screen, form or list they could use instead, and does the first message name the one job it does?
**Smell:** A bot that repackages search, filters or navigation and shows three results where a list shows eighty; comparing many options, a long multi-step purchase or entry of documents and card numbers run as dialogue; an opener such as "How can I help?" or "Ask me anything", or one that leads with a promotion; nothing tells the person the replies are automated or what is out of scope.
**Move:** Scope the conversation to one job the existing surfaces serve badly: few turns, details the person has in their head, nothing sensitive. Open with that job in concrete words, say the replies are automated and what they cannot do, and send browsing, comparing and long data entry to a screen built for it.
**From:** CDG (is conversation the right fit); NN/g site chatbots; NN/g chatbots
